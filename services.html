Computer Help

Slow computer, new computer setup, Windows troubleshooting, basic Mac help, software installation, file transfer guidance, email setup, backups, cleanup, and general questions.

Remote help starts at $65. In-home computer help is $95/hour with a one-hour minimum.

Wi-Fi and Home Networking

Help with slow Wi-Fi, router setup, weak signal, mesh Wi-Fi, devices that drop offline, and basic home network settings. Fiorillo Tech is not an internet service provider.

Wi-Fi and home network visits are $95/hour. Most simple router or device-connection appointments are one to two hours.

Printer Help

Wireless printer setup, printing problems, scanning, drivers, new printer setup, and printer issues that appear after a computer or router change.

Printer help starts at $65 remotely when practical, or $95/hour for an in-home visit.

Phones, Tablets, Email, and Devices

Setup help for email, phones, tablets, streaming devices, smart-home devices, and home-office technology.

Device, email, and home-technology help starts at $65 remotely, or $95/hour on site.

TV Mounting and Home Entertainment

TV wall mounting, smart TV setup, streaming devices, soundbars, input setup, basic picture/audio settings, and practical cable organization.

Standard TV mounting starts at $149 for smaller TVs on standard drywall with a compatible customer-provided mount. Larger TVs and add-ons are priced before scheduling.

TV mounting depends on TV size and weight, wall type, mount compatibility, stud placement, manufacturer requirements, and safe installation conditions. Licensed electrical work and new in-wall electrical wiring are not included.

Very Small Business IT

Computer setup, printer help, Wi-Fi troubleshooting, email guidance, Microsoft 365 questions, backup guidance, vendor coordination, and practical technology advice for small organizations without internal IT staff.

Small-business support is $115/hour with a one-hour minimum. Project pricing is available for larger setup work.

Simple Websites

Clean websites for local businesses, side businesses, clubs, nonprofits, and individuals. This includes small sites, landing pages, domain guidance, hosting guidance, and updates.

One-page starter websites begin at $750. Small 3-5 page business websites begin at $1,500. Larger sites are quoted by scope.

Remote Support
