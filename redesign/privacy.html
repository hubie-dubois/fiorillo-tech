Privacy Policy

When you submit a request, Fiorillo Tech may receive your name, phone number, email address, location, availability, and the details you choose to share.

This information is used to respond to your request, understand the service needed, and schedule or discuss help.

Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, account recovery codes, or highly sensitive personal information through the website form or email. Screenshots can include private information, so please review them before sending.

This policy may be updated as the website or business tools change.
